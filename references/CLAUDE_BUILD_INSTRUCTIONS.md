# Claude Build Instructions — Shama Hotel Website Concept
Read this file and `PREMIUM_HOTEL_WEBSITE_REFERENCES.md` before editing any code.

## Objective
Redesign the Shama Lakeview Asoke Bangkok concept as a credible, premium serviced-apartment hotel website. The previous iteration felt generic and too minimal. Use the reference brands for layout and interaction ideas, not to clone their visual identity.

## CTA ownership — important
The fixed bottom CTA is an advertisement for **Beyond Branding Studio** at https://thebeyondbranding.com. It is not a Shama guest offer.

Required CTA messaging:
- Brand: Beyond Branding
- Service: Hospitality website redesign / brand experience
- Promotional line: “Save 25% on your hotel website redesign”
- Supporting qualifier: “For a limited number of hospitality projects · Enquire for details”
- Actions: “Explore our work” and “Let’s talk”
- Both actions should link to https://thebeyondbranding.com
- Visual treatment: strong red creator/agency bar, clear typography, small and tasteful brand treatment, no fake client logos.
- Responsive behavior: compact on mobile; keep the main CTA visible and ensure page content can scroll above it.
- Do not place Shama booking offers, Shama logos, or “Book now” hotel actions inside the Beyond Branding agency bar.
- Do not describe the 25% offer as an official Shama promotion.

## Hotel website content
Use the official Shama site for facts:
- Main: https://www.shama.com/lakeview-asoke
- Accommodation: https://www.shama.com/lakeview-asoke/accommodation
- Offers: https://www.shama.com/lakeview-asoke/special-offers

Prioritise real property imagery and the actual Shama logo. Stock imagery may only be used temporarily and must be clearly marked as placeholder. Do not invent room claims, awards, reviews, client logos, rates, or booking availability.

## Visual quality bar
- Use EDITION for cinematic brand storytelling.
- Use Aman and Rosewood for editorial typography and image pacing.
- Use Four Seasons for information architecture and booking clarity.
- Use citizenM for practical room and booking UX.
- Use Ace Hotel / The Hoxton for sense of place and local content.
- Reference URLs are in `PREMIUM_HOTEL_WEBSITE_REFERENCES.md`.

## Page structure
1. Hotel header with logo and familiar navigation.
2. Large, high-quality hero image, concise headline, primary booking CTA.
3. Functional booking handoff. A visual form is not a real integration.
4. Room categories with approved photos, actual names and sizes.
5. Facilities and serviced-apartment features.
6. Current official offers, with qualifying terms.
7. Neighbourhood/location content.
8. Photo gallery and practical guest information.
9. Detailed footer.
10. Separate fixed Beyond Branding CTA bar described above.

## Booking integration rule
Do not claim dates or guest selections are passed to Shama unless the official booking engine's documented URL or integration supports it. If it cannot be integrated, clearly state that the form is a concept and send users to the official booking page.

## Final QA
- Verify the CTA bar remains visible on mobile and desktop.
- Confirm it links to thebeyondbranding.com, not Shama.
- Ensure the page has enough bottom padding to prevent content being hidden.
- Check mobile navigation, keyboard focus, contrast, image loading and all external links.
- Do not leave malformed CSS, duplicate rules, or placeholder assets presented as real.
- Keep the “independent design concept” note until client approval.
