# Premium Hotel Website References for Shama Lakeview Asoke
Prepared for Beyond Branding Studio · October 2026

Purpose: study real premium hospitality brands and extract relevant design patterns for a serviced-apartment property. These are references, not templates to copy. Use only Shama-approved brand assets and photography in the concept.

## Priority references

### 1. EDITION Hotels
Website: https://www.editionhotels.com/
Agency case study: https://www.kingandpartners.com/work/edition-hotels/

**Study:** cinematic art direction, contemporary luxury, editorial storytelling, food and beverage as part of the hotel experience, and property pages that can scale across a collection. The agency case study documents EDITION's video-led brand experience and reusable page layouts. Source: https://www.kingandpartners.com/work/edition-hotels/

**Adapt for Shama:** real property photography, a more developed hero, well-structured rooms and facilities, and a distinctive but practical browsing journey.

### 2. Aman
Website: https://www.aman.com/

**Study:** restrained typography, calm spacing, photography-led storytelling, clear destination discovery, and premium pacing.

**Adapt for Shama:** borrow the discipline and photo quality, not the extreme minimalism. Shama needs more practical information and visible booking access.

### 3. One&Only
Website: https://www.oneandonlyresorts.com/

**Study:** immersive resort imagery, emotional storytelling, and high-end destination presentation.

**Adapt for Shama:** large hero image and carefully art-directed property imagery, while keeping the booking path easy to find.

### 4. Rosewood Hotels & Resorts
Website: https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/

**Study:** heritage-led luxury, editorial typography, strong property identity, and a balance between brand storytelling and practical details.

**Adapt for Shama:** use the property's neighbourhood and apartment-style living as a story, not just a grid of amenities.

### 5. Four Seasons
Website: https://www.fourseasons.com/

**Study:** information architecture across many properties, booking utility, room details, offers, location and service information.

**Adapt for Shama:** the clearer conversion flow and content hierarchy, especially on mobile.

### 6. Belmond
Website: https://www.belmond.com/

**Study:** narrative-rich destination content, photography and distinct property personalities. Belmond was named the 2026 World Travel Tech Awards winner for World's Best Luxury Hotel Brand Website. Source: https://worldtraveltechawards.com/award/world-best-luxury-hotel-brand-website

**Adapt for Shama:** make Asoke and Bangkok part of the story, but keep the information useful rather than overly poetic.

### 7. The Berkeley
Website: https://www.the-berkeley.co.uk/

**Study:** strong visual opening, luxury tone, and booking CTA integrated with the hero.

**Adapt for Shama:** one clear headline, compelling approved image, and visible Book Now action.

### 8. citizenM
Website: https://www.citizenm.com/

**Study:** functional hotel UX, direct booking, room information, membership value and a modern voice.

**Adapt for Shama:** efficient booking and practical room details without losing the premium feel.

### 9. Ace Hotel
Website: https://acehotel.com/

**Study:** personality, neighbourhood content, food and beverage, events, and editorial voice.

**Adapt for Shama:** connect the property to the neighbourhood and local experiences.

### 10. The Hoxton
Website: https://thehoxton.com/

**Study:** lively brand personality, local area guides, dining, events, and property-specific content.

**Adapt for Shama:** make location and local utility feel intentional, not a generic list of attractions.

## The three most useful references for this project

1. **EDITION** — brand-led cinematic experience and reusable hotel layouts.
2. **Four Seasons** — clear, trustworthy information and booking flow.
3. **citizenM** — practical booking UX and clear room information.

Combine these patterns rather than copying one site's look. Shama is a serviced-apartment hotel, so rooms, apartment sizes, kitchenette/living features, family stays, facilities, location and long-stay usefulness should be more prominent than they would be on a resort-only website.

## Design checklist

- [ ] Use actual Shama logo files, not a recreated logo.
- [ ] Replace every stock/placeholder photo with approved property photography.
- [ ] Keep rooms, facilities, offers, location, gallery and contact easy to reach.
- [ ] Make booking access obvious on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Use current verified offer language only.
- [ ] Do not imply a booking form works if it does not pass values to the official booking engine.
- [ ] Use the persistent bottom bar to promote **Beyond Branding Studio**, not to masquerade as a Shama discount banner.
- [ ] Label the page as a speculative concept until the property approves it.
